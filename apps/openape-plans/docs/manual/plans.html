Write a plan

Plans are markdown documents your team works from

Writing a plan

Plans belong to a team and are visible to everyone in it.

1 Give the plan a title and a body

The body is markdown — headings and lists render in the plan view.

2 Select Create plan

The plan opens with its rendered markdown.

Finding it again

Plans stay with their team.

1 Open the team

Every plan of the team is listed with its status.
